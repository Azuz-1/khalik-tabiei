Evidence: [bundle measurements](evidence/release/bundle-sizes.json) and [capacity report/raw JSONL](release-20260930-security-capacity.md).

Practical measured maximum was 500 rooms/1,500 sockets; room 501 rejected cleanly and existing clients remained responsive. Explicit closure reached zero rooms and socket leases. RSS staying at a high watermark is not proof of unbounded growth or full reclamation. Full-game resource soak and 4,000-socket/ten-player saturation were not performed. The default 64-per-IP limit and 4,000 global limit independently constrain use; 500 ten-player rooms cannot fit simultaneously.

The requested gates are **not weakened**: local tap ≤100 ms, authoritative action p95 ≤500 ms, phase propagation p95 ≤500 ms, and recovery ≤5 seconds after connectivity returns need a declared normal-network/device profile. They were not comprehensively measured. The silent-heartbeat threshold is 25 seconds checked every ten seconds (roughly 25–35-second detection while foreground); existing retry backoff can reach eight seconds. Detection/backoff does not establish compliance with the five-second recovery budget and needs real-profile measurement/policy review. No responsiveness launch gate is declared passed from loopback PING alone.
